## Convex Optimizer Defaults

- **Maximum single-instrument weight** 15%
- **Covariance window and shrinkage** 120 days and 10%
- **Risk aversion** 4
- **L1 turnover regularization** 750 bp-equivalent, used as a stability regularizer and separate from realized trading cost
- **Base execution-cost assumption** 3 bps per traded notional
